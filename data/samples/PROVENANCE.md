# Sample data provenance

The real-world inputs I was given for this assignment were **two different
compressor P&IDs** (26-KA-901 "3rd Stage HP Gas Lift Compressor" and
26-KA-902 "3rd Stage HP Gas Export Compressor"), not two revisions of the
same document. The originals are kept at `_source_real_pids/` for reference
and are what the tag numbers, setpoints, and note text below are drawn from.

Because the assignment needs genuine **revision pairs** (same document,
Rev A -> Rev B) with known ground truth to evaluate the delta engine and
chat honestly, I synthesized three pairs per the FAQ's explicit suggestion
("edit a PDF and re-export ... document their provenance"):

| Pair | Format(s) | Derived from | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pair_A_equipment_schedule` | native PDF <-> native PDF | tag numbers, alarm setpoints, and numbered notes from `Lift_Gas_compressor-P_ID.pdf` (26-KA-901) | primary eval pair: additions, removals, modified setpoints |
| `pair_B_valve_notes` | native PDF <-> native PDF | tag numbers, PSV setpoints, and numbered notes from `Export_Gas_Compressor-P_ID.pdf` (26-KA-902) | second eval pair, independent content |
| `pair_C_cross_document` | **scanned** PDF <-> native PDF | Rev A/B content of `pair_A`, with Rev A rasterized to an image-only PDF (no text layer) via `scripts/generate_samples.py::make_scanned_variant` | demonstrates the OCR (`pdf_scanned`) adapter and the real-world "a scanned as-built supersedes a drawing" scenario named in the assignment |

Every rendered PDF is generated by `scripts/generate_samples.py` from a
single authored Python content structure per pair (table rows + notes for
Rev A and Rev B). Ground truth deltas in `eval/datasets/*_ground_truth.json`
are derived programmatically from that *same* structure (a set-difference
over the authored rows/notes) -- not hand-transcribed from the rendered
PDF -- so there is no risk of ground truth drifting from what the PDF
actually contains. Re-run with `python3 scripts/generate_samples.py`.

Content is realistic (real tag-numbering conventions, real setpoint units
and magnitudes lifted from the source P&IDs) but the specific "Rev A -> Rev
B" edits (e.g. HH setpoint 245 -> 250 barg, a note's %DP changed, an
instrument decommissioned) are authored, not real engineering changes.
